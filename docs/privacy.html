Beyond Obvious — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Beyond Obvious ("we", "us") operates an automated publishing tool that posts our own short educational videos to our own social media accounts, including TikTok.

What this app does

This application authenticates to the account owner's own TikTok account (via TikTok's official OAuth) and uploads videos that we created, to that same account, on a schedule. It acts only on behalf of the account owner.

Data we access

  • TikTok account authorization. When the owner authorizes the app, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token. These are stored securely as encrypted secrets in our deployment environment and are used solely to upload and publish our own videos. They are never shared with any third party.
  • Basic account info (user.info.basic), used only to confirm the authorized account and the privacy options available to it.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, store, or process any personal data about viewers of our videos.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with advertisers or other third parties.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or analytics beyond the public statistics the platform itself provides to the account owner.

Content

All videos published are original content created by Beyond Obvious. Narration is synthetic (AI text-to-speech). Videos are labeled as AI-assisted where the platform provides that option.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as the automation is in use and can be revoked by the account owner at any time from their TikTok account settings (Security & permissions → Manage app permissions), which immediately stops this app's access.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact the account owner through the Beyond Obvious profile.
